# Evidence decisions

Research: before first search; carry in reader/judge prompts and community-derived QA. Detail: `references/research/source-grading.md` 1-6 (lookup). `python3 scripts/ux_research.py gate` computes statuses; agents never write them. Missing gate: manual unverified assessment, no pass/composable audience.

## 1. Fit and caps

Existence: segment_exists, condition_presence, context_of_use, task, pain_point, workaround. Size: segment_share, device_mix, condition_share, prevalence. Population: user_preference, real_user_preference, discomfort. Product_behavior needs observed same-scope completed-run results; intended_behavior needs official intent. Pain reports prove reported existence only.

A direct fit; B needs independent support; C corroborates B across channels; D context/lead; E unfit. Families: measured/statistical/estimate/intent/voice/news/expert. LLM/synthetic: E. News: secondary, no auto-export. Device cues cannot support device_mix.

Apply one-grade steps for missing publication date, secondhand, unclear context, authorized-member access; then lowest caps. Confirmed promotion/seller tie: D. Incentivized preference/praise/counter use: D; pain/workaround B to C, never raise D/E. Seller notice/points cannot cap whole pages. Own-brand needs independent eligible corroboration across channels. Snippets/teasers/summarizing fetches/modelled community demographics: D. Virtual/stopped/deleted/withdrawn/uncleared injection: E. Flags need content-bound review. Uncertain polarity cannot support praise/counters; seller-board silence is no counter. Missing disclosure/내돈내산 prove no independence.

## 2. Independence and denominators

People: run-salted author key; anonymous/default/partial-IP: thread key. Unknown: no unit. Institution: producer/normalized aliases/domain, not portal. Copies/shared identities: one component, best grade, no two-family credit. Repeated: two people. Corroborated: two units across two channel families, or two units with measured/statistical B; one measured A suffices.

Author/thread cap: three sources/five excerpts/run including context. Quotes: 300 characters, five/resource. Never join keys across runs or collect personal histories.

Size/population: observed quantitative support, neutral analytics/stated-n sample/defined population, period/method/product relation. Post/review/like/complaint counts/star averages/self-selection are no denominator. Voice numeric support: hard error. Population statistics: bounds. Estimates: product/date. No invented demographics/weights/frequency. Unknown shares: null/unknown.

## 3. Status, counter and time

Conflict/currency precede support, also for product. Gate outcomes: conflicting (unresolved counter); stale (current evidence old/unknown or missing release date); needs_product_check (no eligible product observation); supported_high (eligible product observation, A, or corroborated B units); supported_medium (two B people one family, independent B+C across families, one statistical B organization); single_voice (one B person/thread); lead_only (empty/no eligible A/B or lone other institution); unfit (nonempty support all E).

Only supported_high/medium motivate community P0/P1 or eligible exports. Other cited statuses: unverified/exploratory/annex. Product failures keep impact severity. Pass proves process/links only.

Counter-search: primary segments/high stakes/shares/weights/P0-P1 motivation. Record completed call IDs, registered read sources/counters, summary, none_found/qualifies/contradicts. None found covers only search; qualifies needs typed scope resolution; contradicts needs content-bound narrower-claim resolution. Keep predecessor/counters; prose cannot erase conflict.

published_at: publication; observed_at: reading; valid_at/version: currency. Current: release date/matching evidence; old/unknown: context. Split changed version/platform windows; publication-time disclosure rules. Recheck retention; recapture or purge graph/artifacts. Expired counters need new search.
